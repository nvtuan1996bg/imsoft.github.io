How we work

Small bets, honest numbers

Most game ideas don’t work, and that’s fine. We’d rather find out in a week than in six months. So we build something rough, put it in front of players, and let what they do tell us whether to keep going.

  • ✓
    Prototype firstA rough playable build beats a polished design document.
  • ✓
    Let players decideIf people don’t come back the next day, we change the game or move on.
  • ✓
    Keep improving after launchShipping is the start. Updates, fixes, and new content come after.

The path every idea takes

1
IdeaOne sentence: what’s fun about it?
2

3

4

What we care about

Games we’d want on our own phones

A few simple rules we try to stick to, in our own games and in the ones we build for others.

No ad after every tap

Ads should be something players choose, like a rewarded video for an extra life, not a punishment for playing.

Feel matters

Most of the work is in the details: how a tile snaps, how long an animation lasts, what a button sounds like.

Talk to the makers

No account managers. When you email us, you’re talking to the people who actually build the games.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I download your games yet?

Not yet. Our first games are still in development. When one goes live, it’ll be listed on this page with links to the App Store and Google Play.

Can I help test a game before it’s released?

Yes, please! Email tuanngv@imsoft.vn and tell us whether you use iPhone or Android. We’ll send you a test build when one is ready.

Can IMSoft build a game for my company?

Possibly. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll be honest about whether it’s a good fit, and roughly how long it would take.

Do you work with publishers?

Yes. We’re happy to share prototypes and test results, or to build to a publisher’s brief.

Are you hiring?

We’re not always hiring, but we’re always glad to hear from game designers, Unity developers, and artists. Send a portfolio or a game you’ve made to tuanngv@imsoft.vn.

Want to say hello?

A project, a partnership, a job, or feedback on one of our games: we read every message ourselves and reply as soon as we can.

Or email us directly: tuanngv@imsoft.vn
